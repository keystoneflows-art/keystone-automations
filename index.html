How an invoice gets filed. No typing, no guessing.

  1. Invoices collected from email or Drive

    Clients email PDFs, scans or phone photos, or you drop them in a Drive folder. Each one is picked up within a minute.

  2. Fields read, math checked

    AI pulls the supplier, date, number, VAT and total, then checks that subtotal plus tax equals the total.

  3. Filed to your sheet, or flagged

    Clean invoices land in your sheet. Duplicates and anything unclear go to Review, with an alert on your phone.

Questions

What does the free test include?

Send 10 real invoices. I run them through the automation and send back the filled sheet, including anything it flagged.

Whose accounts does it run on?

Yours. It is installed on your own n8n, Make or Google account, so the data and the automation stay with you.

Do you see my passwords?

No. You sign in to each tool yourself on a short screen-share, and you can revoke access at any time.

What if an invoice is read wrong?

Totals are checked against subtotal and tax, and anything that fails goes to a Review folder with an alert.

How is my Google data used?

Only to read the files and emails the automation processes and write results to your own sheets. It is never sold or used for ads, and use of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use. Details in the privacy policy.

Can it write to Xero or QuickBooks?

Not yet as a standard package. Google Sheets, Excel and Airtable are standard; other tools are quoted after the free test.

What does it cost?

The test is free. After it, you get one fixed price for the setup, based on your volume and where the data goes. No surprise invoices.

What if we stop working together?

The automation keeps running on your own accounts. You keep the workflow, the sheet and a short video showing how it works.
