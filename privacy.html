Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Keystone Automations ("we", "us") builds automations that process invoices, emails and leads for businesses. This policy explains what data the service handles, why, and how it is protected. It covers this website and the automations we install for our clients (the "Service").

1. Who we are

Keystone Automations is an independent automation service based in Algeria. Contact: keystone.flows@gmail.com.

2. Data we handle

  • Website form: the name, email, firm and message you send us. They reach our inbox through FormSubmit and are used only to reply to you.
  • Invoices and documents: files our clients' automations process, and the fields read from them (supplier, dates, numbers, amounts, tax).
  • Emails: for the email automation, the sender, subject and text of incoming emails, the category and reply produced, and a log row per email.
  • Leads: for the lead automation, what a person types into the client's form (name, email, phone if asked, message) and the score given to it.

The Service is not designed to collect payment card numbers, passwords or government ID numbers.

3. How we use Google user data

When a client connects Google services, the Service uses Google APIs only to:

  • Gmail: read new emails in the connected inbox, apply labels, create reply drafts and, where the client turns it on, send replies to simple questions;
  • Google Drive: read new files in the folders the client chooses and move them to Processed or Review folders;
  • Google Sheets: read the client's own reference sheets (for example an FAQ) and write log rows to the client's spreadsheets.

We access only the inboxes, folders and spreadsheets used to run the Service. Google user data is used solely to provide the Service to the client who authorized it.

Keystone Automations' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not:

  • sell Google user data;
  • use it for advertising, or to build profiles for advertising;
  • transfer it to third parties, except as needed to provide the Service, to comply with law, or with the client's explicit consent;
  • allow humans to read it, except with the client's consent, for security reasons, to comply with law, or to fix a problem the client reported.

Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models.

4. Service providers

To run the Service we use a small number of processors, each receiving only what its step needs: the automation platform (n8n, Make or Zapier, on the client's account where possible); an AI model provider reached through OpenRouter, to read documents and classify or draft emails; Google (Gmail, Drive, Sheets); Airtable and HubSpot when the client uses them; Telegram, to alert the client when something needs a human; and FormSubmit for this website's form.

5. Retention

  • Processed results stay in the client's own spreadsheets, folders and tools, under the client's control.
  • Form messages are kept in our inbox only as long as needed to answer and follow up.
  • When a client stops the Service, we disconnect access and delete the data we hold for that client within 30 days, unless the law requires otherwise.

6. Security

Access is granted by the client signing in to each tool, never by sharing passwords. Credentials are stored encrypted in the automation platform and are never shared. Access to client data is limited to what is needed to run and support the Service.

7. Your choices

  • Clients can revoke Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Anyone can ask what data we hold about them, or ask us to delete it, by writing to keystone.flows@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Significant changes are sent to clients directly.
