Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Keystone Automations ("we", "us") builds and operates AI assistants that answer direct messages for online shops. This policy explains what data our service handles, why, and how it is protected. It applies to our website and to the assistant we install for our clients (the "Service").

1. Who we are

Keystone Automations is an independent automation service based in Algeria. Contact: keystone.flows@gmail.com.

2. Data we handle

  • Shop data from our clients: product names, prices, stock, delivery terms and similar business information the shop provides to us.
  • Customer messages: the text of messages that the shop's customers send to the shop on Instagram or Facebook Messenger, the sender's platform ID, and the assistant's replies.
  • Google Sheets data: the contents of the specific spreadsheets the shop owner authorizes us to use (see section 3).

We do not ask for, and the Service is not designed to collect, payment card details, passwords or government ID numbers.

3. How we use Google user data

When a shop owner connects Google Sheets, the Service uses the Google Sheets API to:

  • read the shop's product and business-information sheets, so the assistant can answer customer questions accurately;
  • write a log row for each conversation the assistant handled (time, message, reply, intent) to the same spreadsheet, so the owner can review it.

We access only the spreadsheets used to run the assistant. Google user data is used solely to provide this Service to the shop that authorized it.

Keystone Automations' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not:

  • sell Google user data;
  • use it for advertising, or to build user profiles for advertising;
  • transfer it to third parties, except as needed to provide the Service, to comply with law, or with the owner's explicit consent;
  • allow humans to read it, except with the owner's consent, for security reasons, to comply with law, or to fix a problem the owner reported.

4. Service providers

To run the assistant we use a small number of processors: Meta (Instagram and Messenger platforms) and SendPulse to receive and send messages, an AI model provider (via OpenRouter) to generate replies from the message and the shop's catalog, Google (Sheets) for the shop's data, and Telegram to alert the shop owner when a customer needs a human. Each receives only what is needed for its step.

5. Retention

  • Short conversation memory (the last few messages, so replies stay in context) is deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Conversation logs stay in the shop's own spreadsheet, under the shop's control.
  • When a client stops the Service, we disconnect access and delete the data we hold for that shop within 30 days, unless the law requires otherwise.

6. Security

Access credentials are stored encrypted in our automation platform and are never shared. Access to client data is limited to what is needed to operate and support the Service.

7. Your choices

  • Shop owners can revoke Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Anyone can ask us what data we hold about them, or ask us to delete it, by writing to keystone.flows@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated to our clients directly.
